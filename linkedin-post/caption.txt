A SEBI circular changes. What should a firm's compliance team do next?

Zayed Jawaid and I built SEBI-REGTECH, a regulatory compliance prototype that turns SEBI circulars into structured obligations teams can search, review and track. We originally built it for a SEBI hackathon.

The goal: give a team a clearer view of what it needs to do, what evidence it needs, and what needs attention when the source changes.

Explore the nine actual website screenshots:

1. Overview
A snapshot of extracted obligations, risk labels, recorded progress and review gaps. The screenshots use saved demo data, not a firm's compliance results.

2. All Obligations
Search and filter by frequency, risk and department. Each record includes a required action, expected evidence, source clause and quoted text.

3. Trust & Verification
Deterministic Python checks match extracted quotations against the source PDF. The saved run contains 299 obligations: 277 grounded, 14 partial matches and 8 flagged for review. The 97.3% figure measures source traceability, not extraction accuracy or completeness.

4. What's Due
Organises recurring, event-driven, ongoing and one-time obligations. It shows stated time limits when extracted, rather than inventing calendar dates.

5. My Register
Filter by firm type, update an obligation's status and save progress. Review flags highlight missing evidence descriptions or missing deadline fields for triggered obligations.

6. What Changed
Compares extracted obligations across circular versions and shows before/after text. Local semantic matching helps recognise related obligations even when their IDs or wording differ. Change candidates still need human review.

7. Automation
Checks SEBI's public listings. With local Ollama running, the update pipeline can download and process a new Investment Adviser circular, verify it and refresh the outputs.

8. Pipeline Operations
Shows the processing stages and generates structured rule objects with triggers, evidence requirements and breach-review actions for a future enforcement integration.

9. Audit Trail
Records pipeline events in a SHA-256 hash chain and checks the chain for modifications.

Built with Python, Streamlit, Pydantic, pdfplumber, RapidFuzz, sentence-transformers and Qwen2.5 via Ollama. Extraction runs locally.

The current end-to-end workflow focuses on Investment Advisers. It is a prototype supporting human compliance review.
